Putty — end-user embed example

Hosting ../lib/putty.js myself and pasting the Export Code “Embed snippet”, exactly as a developer would.

Two wordmarks: a tuned Instrument Serif (anatomy-deform, heavy weight + moved handles) and the Rubik Bubbles bubbly preset (outline-deform).

Instrument Serif — anatomy-deform (heavy weight, two handles moved)

Engine: Putty.AnatomyDeformWordmark.fromState(state). Drag a handle on the right to mutate the letterform.

Rubik Bubbles — outline-deform (bubbly)

Engine: Putty.DeformableOutlineWordmark.fromState(state) (needs opentype.js). Drag an axis handle to deform the outline.
